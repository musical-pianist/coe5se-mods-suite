# Jazzy Cheats

[All mods](../README.md#mods) | [CoE5SE](../CoE5SE/README.md) | [Jazzy Game Presets](../JazzyGamePresets/README.md) | [Jazzy Hotkeys](../JazzyHotkeys/README.md) | [Jazzy Restart](../JazzyRestart/README.md) | [Jazzy UI Tweaks](../JazzyUITweaks/README.md) | [Jazzy AI Observer](../JazzyAIObserver/README.md)

Give yourself resources, drop units anywhere you've seen, get your movement back, and see every plane for a turn. All from the Game Menu.  
**Needs [CoE5SE](../CoE5SE/README.md) from Nexus Mods.**

## What It Adds

Press F10 on the map: a new *Cheats* section sits under Saving & Exiting. Works in hotseat multiplayer though I don't know why you'd want it to.

![](https://staticdelivery.nexusmods.com/mods/10486/images/6/6-1792789881-1313279283.png)

### Resources

- Pick *10* or *100*, then click a resource. Click again for more.
- All 14 resources, current amounts.

![](https://staticdelivery.nexusmods.com/mods/10486/images/6/6-1792789881-1824070782.png)

### Units

- Your class's units (recruits, start army, standard troops), plus whatever your commanders can summon with their rituals.
- Pick *1* or *10*, click a unit, then click a square on the map. Done.
- Search by name. 8 units a page.
- Only squares you've seen. Not in the middle of a fight (just spawn a commander and move units), and not on another player's army unless you're on the same team.
- Right click or Esc cancels.

![](https://staticdelivery.nexusmods.com/mods/10486/images/6/6-1792789886-1461746441.png)

### Restore movement

- Click a square and every unit on it gets its action points back.

Note: A commander who walked into a battle stays locked.

### Reveal Map

- Every plane in full view until your next turn, planes from other mods included. Same as the Game Over screen's *Reveal Map*.

Note: Next turn the fog comes back.

## Limits

No cheats in network games. CoE5SE runs no scripts there.

## For Modders

Jazzy Cheats is a Lua mod. The script, JazzyCheats.lua, is in the mod's folder to read and learn from.
